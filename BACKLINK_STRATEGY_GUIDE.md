## 📍 1. Product Hunt Launch (DR 90+ Backlink)
- **Website**: [ProductHunt.com](https://www.producthunt.com)
- **Action**: Create a free maker account and submit RemoteJobDesk as a product.
- **Details**:
  - **Title**: `RemoteJobDesk`
  - **Tagline**: `Free AI-Powered Work From Home Job Aggregator across 22+ Countries`
  - **Website Link**: `https://remotejobdesk.com`
